# Day <n> — <Topic>

Plan for the day (about 1.5–2 h):

1. **Video <n>A — theory** (`Day <n>A - <Topic> - theory.mp4`, ~<m> min)
2. **Video <n>B — worked examples & practice** (`Day <n>B - <Topic> - worked examples.mp4`, ~<m> min).
   Pause at each pause sign and solve before watching the answer.
3. **Practice sheet** (`day<nn>-practice.pdf`): <k> questions, ~45 min, no notes. Then check the
   answer key, read the solutions for missed questions, and fill in the self-check table.

Subtitles are embedded in both videos (select the subtitle track) and also provided as `.srt` files.
Chapters are embedded too; titles, descriptions and chapter lists are in the `.info.md` files.

## Exam-frequency badges

Every worked example, pause question and practice question shows how many of the <N> past exam
papers (<list>) contain that question type: high = at least half of the papers, medium = at least a
quarter, low = fewer. The counts and the past-exam question numbers are listed at the end of the PDF.
